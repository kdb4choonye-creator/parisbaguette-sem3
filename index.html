Assistant Store Manager / Store Manager

Job Summary :
The Assistant Store Manager / Store Manager bears the overall responsibility for the profitability of the chain restaurant outlet. He/she performs outlet-level support functions, such as customer service, scheduling, handling day-to-day operations, cashiering, closing of the till, ordering inventory, and back office support.

Roles & Responsibilities :
Manage, direct and coordinate all operation-related activities in a proficient and pleasant method.
Ensure overall store image is maintained and the brand is always well represented.
Ensure that the outlet budget is strictly adhered to and that all costs are controlled including shop expense, cost of goods & cost of labour.
Motivate, guide and train the staff in upselling the food and beverage items.
Attend to guest requests and attend to guest complaints as required.

Requirements :
Possess effective management skills
Possess capability to lead, motivate and manage subordinates
Good command of spoken English
PC literate with knowledge in MS Office
Min. Diploma/Degree in F&B management or any related fields
At least 6 years of experience management in Food and Service Operations

Baker / Senior Baker

Job Summary :
The Baker / Senior Baker is responsible for making a wide range of breads, pastries, and other baked goods. This includes ensuring all baking equipment and baking area is clean prior to and post production of any baked items, and ensuring compliance with regulations.

Roles & Responsibilities :
Prepare, bake and present all baked items effectively and efficiently, meeting the company operations manual and gold standards.
Assist in the preparation, baking and presentation of all baked items.
Ensure that procedures for suppliers, equipment, and work areas are complying to the established standards.
Ensure that food hygiene and safety standards are maintained and comply.
All other ad-hoc duties given by Supervisors.

Requirements :
Baking / Pastry preparation
At least a technical certificate in Culinary Art/ Baking / Pastry or any related fields
At least 1-2 years of experience working in kitchen as baker or/& baking preparation.

Cake Artist

Job Summary :
The Cake Artist is responsible for preparing, baking and decorating cakes and other dough or pastry-based confectioneries. This includes the process from planning the products, weighing and measuring ingredients making various baked goods, and finally glazing or decorating them

Roles & Responsibilities :
Prepare, bake and present pastry/ cake effectively and efficiently, meeting the company operations manual and gold standards.
Prepare a wide variety of goods such as whole cakes etc. following traditional and modern recipes as per SOP.
Decorate pastries/ cakes using different icings, toppings etc. to ensure the presentation will be beautiful and exciting.
Monitor stocks for baking ingredients such as flour, sugar etc. and make appropriate orders within budget.
Execute any other duties as assigned.

Requirements :
Pastry preparation or baking
At least a technical certificate in Culinary Art/ F&B or any related fields
At least 1-2 years of experience working in kitchen as a cake artist or/& baking.

Execute any other duties as assigned.

Food preparation or/and cooking
At least a technical certificate in Culinary Art/ F&B or any related fields
· At least 1-2 years of experience working in kitchen as a cook or/& food preparation.

Hospitality Specialist

acting with a customer comes first attitude to create meaningful customer connections. Models and
acts in accordance with company’s guiding principles.

Supports company sales through engagement with customers, suggestive selling and sharing product knowledge.
Assists store with other duties which includes, but not limited to stocking of merchandize, ensuring tidy arrangement of tables and chairs, general cleanliness of the store, etc.

Supports the implementation of company’s programs or initiatives.

Enthusiastic with a positive and patient attitude and possess a flair for customer service.
